--- 2. zły format - Basic

--- 3. Bearer bez tokenu

{"error":{"code":"UNAUTHORIZED","message":"Authorization header must have format: Bearer <token>","requestId":"6c76a551-8578-4a14-be17-4aacf4b09fa9"}}  [401]
